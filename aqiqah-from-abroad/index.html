Questions

How do I pay from abroad?

After you send your order, we confirm the details on WhatsApp and share the payment options for your country.

When will the aqiqah be done?

On the date you choose wherever possible. If the date is very close, tell us on WhatsApp and we will prioritise it.

What proof will I get?

With Medium and Large, a short video of the slaughter is sent to you on WhatsApp within 24 hours, along with photos of the meat being distributed with a card showing your baby's name.

Where does the meat go?

By default, to orphans, madrasa students and poor families in Dhaka. You can also have it delivered to your family in Dhaka instead.

Why two goats for a boy?

The sunnah is two goats for a boy and one goat for a girl, so a boy's aqiqah costs double.
