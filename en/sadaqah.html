Sadaqah

For the sake of Allah, a smile on the needy.

Buy a goat as Sadaqah — we slaughter it according to the Shariah and distribute all the meat to the poor. You receive video proof.

كَمَثَلِ حَبَّةٍ أَنبَتَتْ سَبْعَ سَنَابِلَ
"The example of those who spend in the way of Allah is like a seed that sprouts seven ears, in each ear a hundred grains."
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:261
Sevenfold Reward

One seed, seven hundred grains.

Allah multiplies the reward of Sadaqah up to seven hundred times. When the meat from one goat feeds ten families, that single act of giving returns multiplied beyond measure. You just make the intention — we handle the rest.

مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ
"Charity does not decrease wealth."
Sahih Muslim 2588
Wealth Grows, Never Shrinks

Giving never decreases your wealth.

Many worry that giving means having less. But the Prophet ﷺ said clearly — charity never decreases wealth. Instead, Allah places barakah in it. Let your Sadaqah goat be a source of blessing in your rizq.

كُلُّ امْرِئٍ فِي ظِلِّ صَدَقَتِهِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ
"Every person will be in the shade of their charity on the Day of Resurrection."
Musnad Ahmad 17333
Shade on the Day of Judgment

Your charity will be your shade that Day.

On the Day when the sun is brought close, every person will stand in the shade of their charity. Today's small act of giving becomes tomorrow's great shelter. One goat — bringing the joy of meat to a family that rarely has it — could be your shade.

الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمْوَالَهُم بِاللَّيْلِ وَالنَّهَارِ
"Those who spend by night and by day, secretly and publicly — their reward is with their Lord."
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:274
By Night and By Day

Any time, any amount.

Sadaqah has no fixed time, amount, or occasion. Allah says — those who spend by night and by day, secretly and publicly, their reward is with their Lord. Give a goat today — we'll deliver the meat to the poor on your behalf.
