Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who we are

Aqiqah Sheba (“we”, “us”) arranges aqiqah, sadaqah and kaffarah on behalf of families in Bangladesh and abroad. We are based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with an office in Dhanmondi and a farm in Keraniganj. This policy explains what personal information we collect through aqiqahsheba.com and our WhatsApp service, and how we use it.

Questions or requests about your information: email support@aqiqahsheba.com, or WhatsApp or call us on +880 1733-311019.

Information you give us

When you place an order, ask a question or pay, we collect:

  • Your name, phone or WhatsApp number, and email address (if you choose email instead of WhatsApp)
  • The country you live in
  • Your baby's name (optional), the child's gender and your preferred aqiqah date
  • Your delivery address, if you choose home delivery in Dhaka
  • Your order details: package, number of goats or kaffarahs, and the amount
  • Anything you tell us in WhatsApp messages, calls or emails

We do not see or store your full card or bank details. Payments are handled by the payment provider you use (such as bKash, a card processor, PayPal, Wise, Payoneer or your bank).

Information collected automatically

Our website uses Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads. With your permission where the law requires it, these use cookies and similar technology to record things like the pages you visit, the device and browser you use, your approximate location, and whether you arrived from one of our ads.

Visitors in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland are asked first through a cookie banner. If you choose “Reject”, Google tags run in a restricted mode that does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your choice is saved in your browser; clearing your browser's site data lets you choose again.

The site also reads your device's time zone to show prices in your local currency and to pre-select your country code. This stays on your device.

How we use your information

  • To process, schedule and carry out your order, and to deliver meat or distribute it on your behalf
  • To contact you about your order and send you proof (video and photos) once it is done
  • To arrange and confirm payment
  • To answer your questions
  • To understand which ads and pages bring people to us, and to improve our website and advertising
  • To keep business and accounting records and meet legal obligations

When you place an order, the phone number and email you give us are also sent to Google Ads in encrypted (hashed) form, so Google can match the order to an ad you clicked. This is called enhanced conversions. It is only used where you have allowed it if you are in the UK, EEA or Switzerland.

Legal bases (UK, EEA and Swiss visitors)

  • Contract: to take and fulfil your order
  • Consent: for analytics and advertising cookies and enhanced conversions; you can withdraw it at any time
  • Legitimate interests: to run and improve our service and keep it secure
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial records

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Google, which stores our order records (Google Sheets) and provides Google Analytics and Google Ads
  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us
  • Payment providers you choose to pay with
  • Our staff and delivery team, who need your name, phone and address to deliver your order
  • Authorities, if the law requires it

These services may store information on servers outside your country, including outside Bangladesh, the UK and the EU. They are large providers with their own security and privacy safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as we need it to deliver your order and to keep business and accounting records, then delete it. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for marketing measurement. You can change your cookie choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data for aqiqahsheba.com. To make a request, email support@aqiqahsheba.com or contact us on WhatsApp.

If you are in the UK, EEA or Switzerland and are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our service is used by parents and guardians. We only collect a child's name and gender when a parent gives it to us for their child's aqiqah.

Security

We limit access to order information to the people who need it, and our records are kept in password-protected accounts. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
