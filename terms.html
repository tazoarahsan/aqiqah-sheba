How ordering works

Sending an order through our website or WhatsApp is a request. Your order is confirmed once we reply to confirm the details and you complete payment. We may decline an order, for example if we cannot meet the requested date.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are fixed in Bangladeshi taka (৳) and include everything listed for the package.
  • Amounts shown in other currencies are approximate. They include about 3% to cover international transaction costs. We confirm the exact amount before you pay.
  • We share payment details after confirming your order. Accepted methods include bKash, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Wise, Payoneer and bank transfer.
  • A boy's aqiqah is two goats and costs double the single-goat price.

The goat and the aqiqah

  • Goat sizes are live-weight ranges (Small 15–19 kg, Medium 20–25 kg, Large 26–30 kg). The meat you receive is less than the live weight, because bones, skin and other parts are removed.
  • We choose healthy animals that meet the Islamic requirements for sacrifice.
  • The slaughter is carried out according to Islamic rules, with the intention made in your child's name.
  • Numbers such as “children served” are estimates based on typical meat yield.

Dates and scheduling

We aim to perform the aqiqah on your preferred date. In rare cases, such as an animal health issue, severe weather or a public holiday, we may need to move it. If so, we will tell you and agree a new date with you.

Delivery and distribution

  • Home delivery is only available within Dhaka City. Please make sure someone is available to receive the meat.
  • If you choose distribution, we give the meat on your behalf to orphans, madrasa students and poor families in Dhaka, as we judge best.
  • We send proof (a short video and photos) on WhatsApp, or by email if you do not use WhatsApp.

Kaffarah and sadaqah

For kaffarah, we feed the number of people your payment covers, on your behalf. Deciding whether you owe kaffarah, and how many, is your responsibility; please ask a scholar you trust. Information on our website is general guidance, not a religious ruling (fatwa).

Cancellations and refunds

  • If you cancel before we have bought or set aside an animal for your order, we refund your payment in full, minus any payment transaction fees.
  • Once an animal has been bought for you, or the aqiqah, sadaqah or kaffarah has been carried out, we cannot offer a refund.
  • If we cannot carry out your order, we will refund you in full.
