Yes, fully. The goat is faced towards the qibla, "Bismillahi Allahu Akbar" is recited, and a sharp blade severs the trachea, oesophagus, and the two major blood vessels in a single clean motion — exactly as the sunnah prescribes. A short video is sent to you via WhatsApp as proof. The method has been reviewed by qualified ulama in Bangladesh. Method basis: Sahih Bukhari 5503, Sunan Abu Dawud 2839
The sunnah is two goats for a boy and one goat for a girl. This is narrated from Aisha (RA) that the Prophet ﷺ said: "Two similar goats for a boy and one goat for a girl." Some Hanafi scholars hold that one goat is permissible for a boy if two is not affordable. We offer discounted 2-goat bundles — message us on WhatsApp. Source: Sunan Tirmidhi 1513, Abu Dawud 2834 (Sahih)
The 7th day is optimal — the Prophet ﷺ performed aqiqah for Hasan and Husayn (RA) on the 7th day. If that's not possible, scholars hold that day 14, day 21, or any later day is valid. The reward and significance remain; the aqiqah should not be abandoned just because the optimal window has passed. Source: Sahih Ibn Hibban 5311, Jami Tirmidhi
