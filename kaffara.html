What is kaffarah?

Kaffarah is an expiation: a set act a Muslim must do to make up for certain sins. The two most common are deliberately breaking a Ramadan fast without a valid reason, and breaking an oath sworn by Allah.

How much is kaffarah for Ramadan 2027?

For a fast broken on purpose, the kaffarah is to fast 60 days in a row. Only if you are genuinely unable to do that do you feed 60 poor people instead. We set the cost of feeding one person at ৳300, so one kaffarah for a broken fast is ৳18,000.

«هَلْ تَجِدُ رَقَبَةً تُعْتِقُهَا؟» قَالَ: لاَ، قَالَ: «فَهَلْ تَسْتَطِيعُ أَنْ تَصُومَ شَهْرَيْنِ مُتَتَابِعَيْنِ؟» قَالَ: لاَ، فَقَالَ: «فَهَلْ تَجِدُ إِطْعَامَ سِتِّينَ مِسْكِينًا؟»

"Can you find a slave to free?" He said no. "Can you fast two consecutive months?" He said no. "Can you feed sixty poor people?"

The Prophet ﷺ to a man who broke his Ramadan fast. Sahih al-Bukhari 1936, Sahih Muslim 1111.

Freeing a slave no longer applies today, which leaves fasting first and feeding second.

Which broken fasts need kaffarah?

In the Hanafi school, kaffarah is due when you deliberately break a Ramadan fast by eating, drinking or intercourse, with no valid excuse (al-Marghinani, al-Hidayah). The Shafi'i and Hanbali schools require it only for intercourse (Ibn Qudamah, al-Mughni). Fasts missed for a valid reason, like illness or travel, need no kaffarah; you simply make them up.

Kaffarah does not replace the fast itself. You still make up (qada) every fast you broke.

What if I broke more than one fast?

In the Hanafi school, if you broke several fasts in the same Ramadan and did not pay kaffarah in between, one kaffarah generally covers them (Ibn Abidin, Radd al-Muhtar). Other schools require one kaffarah per fast. Because cases differ, ask a scholar you trust how many kaffarahs you owe, then enter that number in the calculator.

Kaffarah for breaking an oath

If you swear by Allah to do or not do something and then break that oath, the kaffarah is to feed or clothe 10 poor people. If you cannot afford that, you fast three days. At ৳300 per person, kaffarah for a broken oath is ৳3,000.

فَكَفَّارَتُهُ إِطْعَامُ عَشَرَةِ مَسَاكِينَ مِنْ أَوْسَطِ مَا تُطْعِمُونَ أَهْلِيكُمْ أَوْ كِسْوَتُهُمْ أَوْ تَحْرِيرُ رَقَبَةٍ ۖ فَمَن لَّمْ يَجِدْ فَصِيَامُ ثَلَاثَةِ أَيَّامٍ

"Its expiation is feeding ten poor people from the average of what you feed your own families, or clothing them, or freeing a slave. Whoever cannot find the means must fast three days."

Quran, Surah al-Ma'idah 5:89

What is the difference between kaffarah and fidya?

Kaffarah is for a fast broken on purpose. Fidya is for someone who cannot fast at all and has no hope of making the fasts up, such as the very elderly or the chronically ill. Fidya is feeding one poor person for each fast missed.

وَعَلَى الَّذِينَ يُطِيقُونَهُ فِدْيَةٌ طَعَامُ مِسْكِينٍ

"For those who can fast only with extreme difficulty, a ransom: feeding a poor person."

Quran, Surah al-Baqarah 2:184

When should I pay kaffarah?

As soon as you are able. It can be paid at any time of year, and there is no need to wait for Ramadan.

Frequently asked questions

How much is kaffarah for breaking a fast?

If you are unable to fast 60 days in a row, it is feeding 60 poor people: ৳18,000 per kaffarah, at ৳300 per person.

Is kaffarah compulsory?

Yes. If you deliberately broke a Ramadan fast without a valid reason, or broke an oath, kaffarah is an obligation, alongside sincere repentance.
