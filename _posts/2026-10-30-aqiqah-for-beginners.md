If this is your first child or your first time arranging an aqiqah, the questions can feel overwhelming. This beginner's guide answers them in plain language, one at a time. For more depth on any point, see our [complete guide to aqiqah](/blog/aqiqah-complete-guide/).

## What exactly is aqiqah?

Aqiqah is a sacrifice — a sheep or goat — offered to thank Allah for a newborn and to seek blessing and protection for the child. It is a cherished Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ, performed once for each child.

## When should I do it?

The best time is the seventh day after birth. Count the birth day as day one. If the seventh day has already passed, most scholars agree you can perform it later, so you have not missed your chance.

## How many animals do I need?

Two sheep or goats for a boy, and one for a girl, as the Prophet ﷺ taught:

If you cannot manage two for a boy, one is enough — the Prophet ﷺ is also reported to have offered a ram for each of Hasan and Husayn (RA), which scholars cite to show that one is sufficient while two is more complete.

## What happens on the day?

Three Sunnah acts come together: the sacrifice, naming the child, and shaving the baby's head. Many families also give the weight of the shaved hair in silver as charity.

## What do I do with the meat?

Share it. Keep some for your family, gift some to relatives and neighbours, and give some to the poor. Feeding others is a big part of why aqiqah is so loved.

## Is aqiqah compulsory?

For most scholars it is a strongly recommended Sunnah; in the Hanafi school (common in Bangladesh) it is a commendable act. Either way, performing it is a good and rewarding choice, and a family that genuinely cannot afford it is not sinful.

## How much does it cost?

The cost depends mainly on the price of the animal, plus slaughter and distribution. Prices vary with the market, so it is worth checking current local rates or asking a [trusted service](/en/packages) for a clear, all-inclusive quote.

## Can someone do it for me?

Yes. You can perform the aqiqah yourself, or appoint a trustworthy person or service to do it on your behalf — a completely valid option in Islam, and a practical one for busy or overseas families.

## Your simple next step

Pick the day, decide on the animal, and either arrange the slaughter yourself or book a reliable service. With those basics handled, you will have fulfilled a beautiful Sunnah at the very start of your child's life.
