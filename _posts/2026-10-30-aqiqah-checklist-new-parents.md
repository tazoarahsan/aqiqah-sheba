Performing aqiqah is simpler than many new parents expect. This checklist walks you through every step in order, each one grounded in the Sunnah, so nothing is missed. For the background to each step, see our [complete guide to aqiqah](/blog/aqiqah-complete-guide/).

## 1. Confirm the seventh day

The recommended time is the seventh day after birth. Count the day of birth as day one — so a baby born on a Saturday has their aqiqah the following Friday. This, along with naming and shaving, comes from the hadith of Samurah ibn Jundub (RA):

If the seventh day passes, do not worry: it can be performed later when you are able.

## 2. Decide how many animals

The Sunnah is two comparable sheep or goats for a boy and one for a girl (hadith of Umm Kurz, RA). If two are not possible for a boy, one is sufficient and the aqiqah is still valid.

## 3. Choose a healthy animal

Select a sound, healthy sheep or goat of the correct age, free of obvious defects. The majority of scholars also allow a cow or camel (counting as seven shares). The animal should meet the same standard of soundness as the Eid sacrifice.

## 4. Arrange a proper halal slaughter

The animal must be slaughtered in the correct Islamic manner, with the name of Allah pronounced. If you are using a service, confirm that their slaughter is genuinely halal and that the intention is made on your child's behalf.

## 5. Name your child

Naming is part of the same seventh-day occasion in the hadith above. Choose a good, meaningful name — ideally before or on the day of the aqiqah.

## 6. Shave the baby's head

Shaving the newborn's head is part of the Sunnah of this day. It is then recommended to give the weight of the shaved hair in silver as charity — from what the Prophet ﷺ taught Fatimah (RA) regarding Hasan (RA).

## 7. Distribute the meat

Share the meat in three directions: keep some for your family, gift some to relatives and neighbours, and give some to the poor. There is no fixed obligatory proportion, but reaching the needy is central to the spirit of aqiqah.

## 8. Make du'a for your child

Complete the occasion by asking Allah to protect, guide, and bless your child. This is the heart of the day — gratitude turned into prayer.

## Quick-reference checklist

* Seventh day confirmed (day of birth = day one)
* Number of animals decided (2 for a boy, 1 for a girl)
* Healthy, defect-free animal chosen
* Halal slaughter arranged, name of Allah pronounced
* Child named
* Head shaved; silver given by weight of hair
* Meat distributed to family, friends, and the poor
* Du'a made for the child
